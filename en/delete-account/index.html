How to delete your account and data

In the app: Profile, the “Data” section, the “Delete account” row. Before deletion we will offer to export everything to a file — after deletion there will be nothing to restore.

Write to us at this email with any question about your data: we reply within thirty days, and usually sooner.

What happens next: we delete your profile, results, equipment together with photos, rank and the document, and notes. Your data does not stay with your coach either — there is no reason to keep the data of a deleted account. Deletion takes up to thirty days and cannot be undone. If the law of your country requires it to be done faster, we do it faster.
